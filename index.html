Privacy Policy for Block Cozy Game

Last updated: November 17, 2026

LandN Games Studio ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates Block Cozy Game (Android package: com.landngames.blockcozy). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, or shared when you play the game.

Information We Collect

Block Cozy Game does not require you to create an account. The game does not directly ask you to provide your name, postal address, phone number, or email address in order to play.

The game includes integration with third-party advertising technology, including Google AdMob. Depending on the release version and whether the advertising SDK is initialized, Google or its advertising partners may automatically collect or process:

  • IP address, which can be used to estimate approximate location;
  • device identifiers and advertising identifiers, where available and permitted;
  • information about app interactions and advertising interactions;
  • diagnostic data, such as crash and performance information; and
  • information about advertisements shown or requested by the app.

The precise information processed can vary based on device settings, app version, region, and consent choices. More details are available in Google's privacy documentation linked below.

Advertising

Block Cozy Game includes Google AdMob integration for formats such as interstitial and rewarded advertisements. Depending on the release version and configuration, ads may be disabled, displayed in test mode, or displayed as live advertisements. Test advertisements do not generate advertising revenue.

When Google advertising services are active, Google and its advertising partners may process information for ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security, and prevention of fraud or abuse. Where applicable, ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on consent, location, and settings.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, consent or privacy-choice mechanisms must be provided before applicable advertising-related processing. Depending on the app version and region, consent choices may be presented through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another appropriate consent mechanism.

You can also manage certain advertising privacy settings through your Android device and Google account. For questions about choices available in Block Cozy Game, contact us using the email address below.

Third-Party Services

The game includes Google AdMob integration and related Google advertising technology. Google processes information according to its own policies and terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy

Local Game Data

Game scores, best scores, current game progress, settings, language choices, and visual preferences may be stored locally on your device so that you can resume your game and retain your preferences. We do not use this locally stored gameplay information to directly identify you.

Data Sharing

LandN Games Studio does not sell players' personal information. Where advertising services are used, information may be transmitted to Google and relevant advertising technology partners for the purposes described above. We do not operate a social-sharing or player-account feature that requires you to submit personal contact details.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the game. Third-party services use their own security controls, including transport encryption where applicable. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay data stored locally on your device generally remains there until you clear app data, remove it through available game functions, or uninstall the game, depending on the data involved.

Information processed by third-party services is retained and deleted according to their own applicable policies. To ask about information controlled directly by LandN Games Studio, contact us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Block Cozy Game is a general-audience block puzzle game and is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal contact details through the game. Advertising and data processing are subject to additional protections where applicable law or platform policy requires them. A general-audience content rating does not by itself establish the age of users or eliminate these obligations.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when game features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
